**Vice Nights** turns your photos into GTA-style cover art: a multi-panel poster
with bold caption lettering and a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade.

- **Any photo** — upload up to three photos from your machine (never uploaded
  anywhere), or use the two sample photos. Fill several panels with one photo or
  several, and crop and position each panel: click a panel, then set its photo,
  zoom and pan.
- **Multi-panel poster** — three layouts (Sunset Strip, Four Corners, Vice Trio)
  that adapt to the cover's shape, with crisp white panel frames and a dusk grade.
- **Fast and easy** — photo in, caption typed, one click exports a PNG in
  1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080.
- **Original art** — no Rockstar logo, wordmark, artwork, character or font. The
  lettering is drawn with the system sans-serif stack; the grades are gradients
  drawn on canvas.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. `?shot=cover` and
`?shot=editor` render fixed scenes for screenshots (see `shots.json`).

## Hand it in

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
